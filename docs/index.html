How do I install VibeArc?

Download the APK, then open it on your Android phone. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager. Download only from our GitHub release. Back up your library before updating; don’t uninstall the previous version first.

What should I know before listening?

VibeArc is independent and open source, not affiliated with Google or Last.fm. Online sources and lyrics depend on provider availability. Last.fm needs your own configuration. Only download music you own or have permission to save. Windows is not available yet.
